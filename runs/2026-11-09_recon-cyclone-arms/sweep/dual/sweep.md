# The Cyclone's arms and A: dual

Every combination of three to six arms and A at the tip, three-quarters or half-way out, on every arm or every other, each re-sized for all seven jobs (`recon/cyclone_sweep.py`). Mode 4's wing is the lifting arms (`cyclone.layout`): 3, a 30° dihedral wing and a fin; 4, an X; 5, four lifting and a fin; 6, four lifting 30° either side of level, a fin up and one down. The design today is the first row of the second table.

A is rated for the larger of the spinning peak and its share of mode 1's. The pitch-over from 3 to 4 runs A at its burst, tilted forward only as far as the height holds, so none loses height in it: each starts it from the safety height.

Mode 2 wind: the strongest it holds station in spinning, until the retreating arm stalls, B's swing runs out, or A's rating. One A out in mode 1: the rest at their burst against the weight; above 1 it can still climb. Mode 4 pitch: the least of the two ways A's thrust can be shifted between the motors above and below at the search speed, trim taken off.
