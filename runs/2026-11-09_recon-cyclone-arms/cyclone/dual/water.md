## The launch

A at its burst (1.30 × the weight) with the buoyancy still under it: the arms slide up out of the water edge first and clear it in **0.14 s** at 1.0 m/s. The surface tension along both faces is 0.33 N (1.5% of the weight) and the skin friction less: there is nothing to break free of. Then mode 1 to the clearance.

## Landing

In mode 1 at 1 m/s the arms enter edge first and stop over the draft: 1.6 g. Motors out, the seed fall meets the water at 3.7 m/s: 7.9 g unflared, 1.0 g if the spin is spent on a flare. After a seed fall it lies flat, A's props in the water; B has to stand the arms up before it can launch.

Not modelled: the arms' twist from flat to standing in the water, waves breaking over it, and the hub's seals.
