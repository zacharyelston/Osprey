Situational awareness for a sailboat

Eyes on the mast.
A scout in the air.

Nest puts camera pods on the mast that keep watch over the whole horizon, spot a person in the water and guide the boat into a slip. Recon is the boat's scout: a drone that flies out to look at whatever the captain needs to know about, then comes home to the deck or the sea.

Designed in simulation first. Every number here comes from screening models with stand-in parts, not from hardware.

Recon on the water

Four ways home when the deck is out

A catch on a heeling, pitching deck isn't always possible, so the bird has to float. How it leaves the water again is the choice.

Float plane

A planing shoe with a step under the belly, the prop raised on a pylon. It takes off in 12–18 m, but only in waves of a few centimetres, and lands at 6–8 g.

Adds 0.17–0.33 kg

Parachute to the water

Launched from the deck as now, it comes down under a canopy beside the boat and is picked up with the boathook. The canopy drifts in the wind, and the bird has to be found in the water.

Not modelled yet

Tailsitter

The blended wing stands on four strut pods with its props 56 cm clear of the water. It climbs straight up and pitches over onto the wing in 4.3 s, and settles back onto the pods at 2 g.

Launches up to sea state 3–4

Cyclone

Three wing arms, after Nick Rehm's tricopter. It hovers only by spinning as one rotor, on less than half the tailsitter's power, and flies with two arms up in dihedral and one down as a fin. Motors off, it falls like a seed at 3–4 m/s. Hovering, it can't take much wind.

2.91 kg with the gimbal, against the tailsitter's 2.96

One system

How Nest and Recon work together

  1. Nest keeps the watch. The pods sweep the horizon, track traffic and see a person go over the side, out to about 2 km in sea state 3.
  2. Recon goes and looks. Nest's tracks become Recon's tasks: a datum to search, a vessel to inspect, an anchorage to survey before arrival.
  3. Or they split the circle. With the spreader pairs watching ahead and astern, Recon flies escort and takes the beams.
  4. Nest brings the drone home. The pods' lower eyes see the deck and the drone's final approach.
  5. Recon lightens the mast. If the drone does the long-range looking, the masthead's long zoom can step down, saving about 1.2 kg aloft.

How it's designed

Simulation first, hardware second

Nest models

Wave-driven boat and mast motion, optics with the Johnson criteria and wave masking, ray-cast occlusion by sails and hull, and a tasking sim that hands out looking groups.

Recon sizing

OpenMDAO and OpenAeroStruct size each airframe for each job; a 3-DOF flight sim flies it through. All seven jobs size, calibrate and fly in about 35 s on a laptop.

Synthetic data

MuJoCo renders every camera with metric depth, class masks and exact poses, in the format real captures from the first pod will use.

Every input is a stand-in (a reference 40 ft sloop, typical cameras, electronics, batteries and printed materials). The numbers show which way the design should move; measurements replace them as hardware arrives.
